# Campus Dining App - Course Project Proposal

Course: CS-591 Human-Computer Interaction
Student: final-year M.Sc. candidate
Term: Autumn
Advisor: Prof. M. Okafor

## Project brief
This coursework studies how first-year students discover dining options on
campus and proposes a small mobile prototype to reduce queue times at peak
hours. The project is graded against the course rubric for research rigour,
prototype quality and reflective write-up.

## Deliverables
- A usability study with 12 participant interviews.
- A low-fidelity prototype (Figma) and one interactive flow.
- A final report of 8,000 words with rubric alignment appendix.

## Scope boundaries
No commercial launch or pricing plan is in scope; the artefact is submitted for
course assessment only.
