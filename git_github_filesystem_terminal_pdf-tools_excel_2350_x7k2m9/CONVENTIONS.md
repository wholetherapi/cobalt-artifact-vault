# Vault Conventions

## Project buckets

- `Work/Projects/` - client-facing and commercial deliverables: proposals, pricing
  sheets, launch plans and statements of work prepared for paying clients or
  internal product programmes.
- `School/Graduation_Projects/` - coursework and academic project material: course
  submissions, capstone reports and thesis chapters.

## Intake

New deliverables dropped by collaborators land in `inbox/` and are filed into
the matching project bucket. Choose the bucket by what the artefact is and
who it is written for.

## Tracking

`TRACKING.md` lists open items as `- [ ] **<path>:<line>** - <message>`, where
`<line>` is the exact 1-based line of the marker inside the referenced file.
